## Article Pipeline

### Priority 1

1. איך לבחור קליניקה אסתטית בתל אביב?
   - Search intent: trust and comparison
   - Angle: medical credentials, consultation process, safety, natural results

2. בוטוקס או פילר: מה ההבדל?
   - Search intent: treatment education
   - Angle: mechanism, indications, timing, risks, consultation decision-making

3. מה חשוב לדעת לפני הזרקות אסתטיות?
   - Search intent: pre-treatment preparation
   - Angle: medical history, expectations, contraindications, consent

4. איך מגיעים לתוצאה טבעית בבוטוקס?
   - Search intent: concern about over-treatment
   - Angle: micro-dosing, facial movement, anatomy, follow-up

## Recommended Article Structure

Each article should include:

- Clear answer in the first paragraph
- Medical disclaimer
- Who the treatment is suitable for
- Who should avoid or delay treatment
- What happens during consultation
- Expected timeline and recovery
- Risks and possible side effects
- Results may vary disclaimer
- FAQ section with 4-6 questions
- Reviewed by doctor note
- Last updated date

## GEO Requirements

To increase citation likelihood in AI search:

- Use answer-first paragraphs
- Add FAQPage schema to each article
- Add Article schema with author and reviewer
- Include specific clinical terms but explain them simply
- Cite authoritative medical or regulatory sources when possible
- Keep paragraphs short and extractable
- Update articles every 3-6 months

## Internal Linking Rules

- Every treatment article should link back to the consultation CTA.
- Every article should link to at least one related article.
- The home page should link to the strongest article in each cluster.
- The footer should eventually include links to key treatment pages once they are built.

## Compliance Notes

- Avoid guaranteed outcomes.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as best, safest or permanent unless legally supported.
- Before/after content must include: התוצאות עשויות להשתנות.
- Medical content should state that it does not replace personal medical consultation.
