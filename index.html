Our Doctors

Two perspectives. One treatment philosophy.

Dr. Shmuel Shai Shiloh DMD, MSc

Dr. Shiloh is a graduate of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and Hadassah Medical Center, holding both a DMD degree and an MSc in Medical Big Data.

Dr. Shiloh's philosophy is rooted in precise minimalism — clean, refined, and natural-looking treatments that preserve facial harmony and authenticity.

Every case is planned jointly with Dr. Khoury, combining two clinical perspectives before a treatment begins.

Dr. Yasmine Khoury DMD, MSc

Dr. Yasmine Khoury, a dental physician and biomedical researcher, graduated with honors from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

Dr. Khoury's professional approach focuses on dental aesthetics with a minimalist and precise touch — gentle, clean, and natural treatments.

Every treatment plan is reviewed jointly with Dr. Shiloh, so each decision reflects two medical perspectives, not one.
